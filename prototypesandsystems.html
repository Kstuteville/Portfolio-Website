Combat & Traversal System

Sword Combat & Traversal Prototype

Unreal Engine - Core Player Controls

Goal: Prototype core player controls for an action-adventure/RPG experience, focusing on fluid melee combat and responsive traversal mechanics that feel intuitive and engaging.

Combat System

Three Distinct Sword Attacks
Chained combo system allowing for varied attack sequences and tactical combat flow
Kick Mechanic
Interrupt enemy actions and break guard states for strategic combat advantage
Assassinate Move (Right-Click)
Contextual high-damage strikes for stealth and tactical elimination

Traversal System

Custom Movement Script
Smooth, responsive player control with optimized input handling
Crouch System
Stealth mechanics and enhanced mobility in confined spaces
Vaulting Mechanics
Seamless obstacle navigation to expand exploration and encourage emergent gameplay
Horror Survival System

Adaptive AI & Survival Mechanics

Unreal Engine - Psychological Horror Systems for Requiem Forest

Goal: Build fear through interconnected systems rather than scripted events, creating emergent horror moments through adaptive AI, resource management, and sensory feedback that reward careful planning and spatial awareness.

AI Detection System

Dual-Sense Detection Model
Monster uses both sight detection (triggers full chase when visible) and proximity detection (triggers investigation when close but hidden), allowing effective hiding even at close range
Multi-Sensory Awareness
AI reacts to visual contact and player event triggers, creating realistic awareness that promotes stealth and spatial planning over pure speed
Emergent Encounters
Unpredictable AI behavior ensures each encounter feels unique, avoiding pattern memorization and maintaining tension

Resource Management

Stamina System
Sprinting drains stamina; full depletion causes exhaustion with slower movement and longer recharge times, forcing strategic movement decisions
Flashlight & Battery Management
Essential visibility tool that drains battery over time; players must scavenge batteries throughout environment and strategically toggle usage
Risk vs. Reward Balance
Every resource decision creates tension: use flashlight for safety but risk battery depletion, or sprint to escape but become exhausted

Sensory Feedback Systems

Dynamic Heartbeat Proximity System
Audio cue intensifies as monster approaches: faster and louder heartbeat signals danger without showing the threat directly
Diegetic Warning System
Immersive audio feedback builds psychological tension and spatial awareness, acting as player's primary safety indicator
Contextual Jump Scare
When caught, sudden close-up encounter with intense audio serves as tension payoff, emphasizing fear of failure and heightening chase adrenaline
Procedural Generation System

PCG Dungeon Generation Prototype

Unreal Engine - Dynamic World Building for PVP Combat

Goal: Explore procedural content generation (PCG) through dynamic dungeon creation, building replayable environments for PVP combat scenarios with weighted room distribution and customizable parameters for varied gameplay experiences.

Generation System

Custom Room Array (5 Room Types)
Diverse room templates cycle through randomly, creating unique dungeon layouts each generation
Weighted Room Distribution
Boss rooms spawn once at map end, stair rooms appear less frequently, ensuring balanced dungeon progression
Seed-Based Generation
Save and reproduce preferred map layouts by using specific seeds for consistent dungeon generation

Customization Parameters

Adjustable Room Count
Specify exact number of rooms to control dungeon size and exploration duration
Enemy Population Control
Set enemy density per dungeon, scaling challenge level for different player skill ranges
Boss Room & Exit System
Guaranteed boss encounter at dungeon terminus with AI boss and exit point for victory condition
