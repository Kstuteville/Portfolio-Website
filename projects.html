Research · NYU Tandon

Physics-Based RL Locomotion for Enemy AI

From Robotics to Game Feel

For our AI for Games course at NYU Tandon, we ran a research playtest around a single question: does physics-based reinforcement-learning locomotion make an NPC drone feel more alive?

The work was inspired by Embark Studios' Arc Raiders, where players consistently describe the robot enemies as “feeling smart.” We wanted to find out how much of that impression comes from movement itself rather than decision-making.

We built a Unity platform that lets PPO and PID controllers hot-swap onto the same physics body — same mass, same hit registration, same damage conditions. The only variable is how motor thrust is computed: scripted versus learned. That isolation is what makes the comparison meaningful, since any difference players report has to come from the controller rather than from the encounter design.

Research Question

Does learned locomotion change how alive an enemy feels?

Method

Player-facing playtest comparing PPO against a PID baseline

Platform

Unity, physics-driven drone

Course

GameAffordance-Net

3D Affordance Learning for Game Engines

Research at NYU Tandon into how machines can understand the ways people interact with objects and environments — and how that understanding can be pushed directly into a real-time game engine.

The system uses a DGCNN point-cloud model trained on 8,000+ PartNet examples to predict object parts and their affordances. Python, PyTorch, and Open3D handle point-cloud processing, label generation, training, and evaluation.

Predictions are then integrated into Unreal Engine 5, where they automatically generate interaction points, collision volumes, and gameplay metadata — turning a perception model into usable level-design tooling instead of leaving it as an offline result.

Emotion Aware NPC Brain

A Real-Time Emotion-Aware NPC System for Unreal Engine

Emotional NPC is a real-time, multimodal character system that reads the player’s face, voice, and spoken language to drive emotionally responsive dialogue and behavior. By combining facial-expression recognition, speech-to-text, large-language reasoning, and speech synthesis, the NPC doesn’t just talk back, it adapts. It tracks trust, emotional context, and narrative state to dynamically shape conversations, unlock lore, and assign quests or objectives on the fly based on how the player behaves emotionally. The result is a flexible, living character that evolves with the player, turning moment-to-moment interaction into a core gameplay system rather than a scripted layer.

Real-time demonstration of emotion-aware NPC interaction featuring multimodal AI systems in Unreal Engine

In Active Research & Development

Research Focus

Affective Computing & Interactive Narrative

Platform

Unreal Engine 5

Development

Solo: research, design, and implementation

Status

Modular Framework (Open Source)

Unreal Engine 5 Python DeepFace Whisper STT GPT-4o-mini OSC Protocol Facial Emotion Recognition Machine Learning

Research Question: How does real-time emotional awareness, via facial expression, vocal tone, and natural language, affect player perception of NPC presence, empathy, and narrative immersion in adaptive game worlds?

Why This Matters: Most NPCs operate on static dialogue that ignores the player’s emotional state. This research investigates whether emotionally adaptive NPCs can deepen immersion, drive dynamically personalized storytelling, and foster more meaningful, empathy-driven player choices.

My Final Reflection: For this project, I pushed myself to explore how emotionally responsive NPCs could exist as part of actual gameplay instead of just feeling like a scripted feature. I worked with multimodal threading, real time emotion detection, and playtesting with real users to see how players actually responded to an NPC that could react to their emotions. I also did research into affective computing and how players form relationships with in game characters, and I learned a lot about how emotional feedback can change the way players behave and make decisions. I was really proud of how much I was able to accomplish in such a short amount of time, especially while learning so many new technical systems at once.

A lot of this project was challenging, especially getting multiple systems like facial expression recognition, speech to text, and real time NPC dialogue to work together without breaking. Some things did not work perfectly. Emotion detection could be inconsistent, and sometimes the NPC responses felt too robotic or gave too much information at once. But those moments helped me understand the limits of the system and how important polishing and tuning really are for player experience.

In the end, I created a working prototype of an emotional NPC that can track trust, respond to player emotions, unlock information, and assign quests in real time based on how the player interacts. If I had more time, I would love to run more playtests with different types of players, add full speech emotion recognition, and improve how the NPC remembers past interactions. I would also want to make the system more modular so other developers could easily plug it into their own games. I truly believe this kind of system unlocks a lot of future potential for how NPCs can work in games, and I am excited to keep developing it, improving it, and eventually releasing it so others can build with it too..

Story Synopsis

A child is left in the forest by their parents as a test of faith. The forest is not normal, the fog drifts as though alive, whispers echo through the trees. As you interact with the map and find keys to get out, an ambiguous presence stalks you, reacting to your "sins" and moments of doubt. The game builds toward a choice at the forest's heart: ritual compliance or rebellion, the game leave you questioning what was real and what was fear.

Unreal Engine 5 Psychological Horror Narrative Design Blueprints Environmental Storytelling Sound Design

Core Mechanics I Designed

Movement & Resource Management

Designed stamina system where sprinting drains stamina bar; reaching exhaustion triggers heavy breathing audio and movement penalties. Forces strategic decision-making between hiding and running. Flashlight battery drains during use, requiring players to conserve light and scavenge batteries scattered throughout the environment.

→ View stamina system demo

Adaptive Monster AI System

Built behavior tree with randomized patrol routes, line-of-sight chase mechanics, and sin-triggered ambushes. AI responds to player interactions with "sinful" objects, alerting the monster to hunt in that area. Integrated heartbeat proximity audio system that grows louder and faster as AI approaches, creating spatial awareness through sound design.

→ View AI behavior demo

Narrative System

Developed modular narrative manager that triggers context-aware inner monologue based on player location and interactions. Area-based voice lines activate once per zone, with unique dialogue for object interactions. Built reusable UI system that can be called seamlessly across different game scenarios.

→ View narrative system demo

Leadership & Project Management

As project lead, I coordinated a four-person team across a one-week sprint cycle, managed version control workflows, and directed feature prioritization to meet October 16th 2025 Game Jam deadline. I established clear design pillars, facilitated playtesting sessions, and ensured cohesive integration between environment design, narrative, and core mechanics.

Fruit Ninja IRL

Rethinking Digital Affordances Through Physical Interaction

I loved Fruit Ninja as a kid, so when I saw the arcade version at Dave & Buster's, I expected something more than just the iPad game on a bigger touchscreen. That missed opportunity sparked this project, what if we translated touchscreen gestures into actual physical sword movements, creating a full-body, immersive experience that fundamentally reimagines how we interact with digital games?

Live motion capture demonstration showing real-time sword tracking in Unreal Engine 5

Solo: design and development

Platform

Focus

Interface Design & Affordances

Input Method

Development Time

The Design Challenge

How do you translate instant, precise touchscreen swipes into physical sword movements? The core assignment was to take an existing digital interface and redesign its affordances through a different medium. Rather than making a 1:1 translation, this project explores what happens when you fundamentally change the interaction model.
