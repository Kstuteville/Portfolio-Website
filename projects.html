Iron Labyrinth

Procedural Dungeon PvP | Cinematic Combat | AI-Driven Encounters

Iron Labyrinth is a dynamic Unreal Engine 5 project that merges procedural dungeon generation with cinematic action gameplay. Every run unfolds within an unpredictable labyrinth — no two layouts, enemies, or encounters are ever the same. Players must navigate twisting halls, uncover hidden paths, and survive ambushes as they battle toward the final boss chamber.

Gameplay trailer and procedural generation showcase coming January 2026

In Active Development - January 2026 Release

Genre

Procedural Dungeon Crawler PvP

Platform

PC (Unreal Engine 5)

Development

Solo Developer

Timeline

Fall 2025 - January 2026

Unreal Engine 5 PCG Framework Blueprints Behavior Trees EQS Systems Motion Capture LiveLink Cinematic Sequencer

The project focuses on showcasing PCG systems, enemy AI, and adaptive combat design within a fully modular environment. Each dungeon iteration spawns unique architecture, loot placement, and AI behaviors, creating a constantly evolving challenge that rewards strategic combat and spatial awareness.

Core Features

Procedural Dungeon Generation

Custom PCG blueprints that reshape level flow and enemy placement with every playthrough. Modular environment pieces combine to create unique architectural layouts, ensuring no two runs feel the same.

Cinematic Combat Sequences

Integrated real-time cutscenes that blend motion-captured fighting choreography with gameplay transitions. Seamless camera work brings cinematic intensity to every encounter.

Enemy AI Systems

Behavior trees and EQS-driven decision-making for flanking, pathfinding, and cooperative enemy tactics. Enemies adapt to player behavior and coordinate attacks for dynamic combat scenarios.

Custom Mocap & Facial Animation

LiveLink-based performance capture for expressive character animations and combat realism. Every movement feels grounded and responsive, from sword strikes to enemy reactions.

Design Philosophy: Iron Labyrinth pushes the limits of procedural generation and cinematic interactivity — building a world that feels alive, reactive, and uniquely challenging every time you enter it. The project showcases technical mastery of UE5's PCG systems while maintaining the hand-crafted feel of a cinematic action game.

Emotion Aware Game NPC

A Real-Time Emotion-Aware NPC System for Unreal Engine

Emotional NPC is a real-time interactive character system that reacts to the player's facial expressions, spoken language, and soon, tone of voice. The system uses multimodal emotion sensing (FER + STT + LLM + TTS) to create NPC conversations that feel more natural, responsive, and narratively meaningful.

Research Focus

Affective Computing & Interactive Narrative

Platform

Unreal Engine 5

Development

Research Team + Solo Implementation

Status

Modular Framework (Open Source)

Unreal Engine 5 Python DeepFace Whisper STT GPT-4o-mini OSC Protocol Facial Emotion Recognition Machine Learning

Research Question: How does real-time emotional awareness—through facial expression, vocal cues, and natural language—shape player perception of NPC empathy, presence, and narrative engagement?

Why This Matters: Traditional NPCs rely on fixed dialogue trees that ignore the player's emotional state. This research explores whether emotionally responsive NPCs can improve immersion, increase narrative engagement through adaptive storytelling, support more human-like interaction, and encourage empathy-driven player decisions based on emotional connection.

Requiem Forest

A Psychological Horror Game About Faith and Fear

Story Synopsis

A child is left in the forest by their parents as a test of faith. The forest is not normal, the fog drifts as though alive, whispers echo through the trees. As you interact with the map and find keys to get out, an ambiguous presence stalks you, reacting to your "sins" and moments of doubt. The game builds toward a choice at the forest's heart: ritual compliance or rebellion, the game leave you questioning what was real and what was fear.

Unreal Engine 5 Psychological Horror Narrative Design Blueprints Environmental Storytelling Sound Design

Core Mechanics I Designed

Movement & Resource Management

Designed stamina system where sprinting drains stamina bar; reaching exhaustion triggers heavy breathing audio and movement penalties. Forces strategic decision-making between hiding and running. Flashlight battery drains during use, requiring players to conserve light and scavenge batteries scattered throughout the environment.

→ View stamina system demo

Adaptive Monster AI System

Built behavior tree with randomized patrol routes, line-of-sight chase mechanics, and sin-triggered ambushes. AI responds to player interactions with "sinful" objects, alerting the monster to hunt in that area. Integrated heartbeat proximity audio system that grows louder and faster as AI approaches, creating spatial awareness through sound design.

→ View AI behavior demo

Narrative System

Developed modular narrative manager that triggers context-aware inner monologue based on player location and interactions. Area-based voice lines activate once per zone, with unique dialogue for object interactions. Built reusable UI system that can be called seamlessly across different game scenarios.

→ View narrative system demo

Leadership & Project Management

As project lead, I coordinated a four-person team across a one-week sprint cycle, managed version control workflows, and directed feature prioritization to meet October 16th 2025 Game Jam deadline. I established clear design pillars, facilitated playtesting sessions, and ensured cohesive integration between environment design, narrative, and core mechanics.

Fruit Ninja IRL

Rethinking Digital Affordances Through Physical Interaction

I loved Fruit Ninja as a kid, so when I saw the arcade version at Dave & Buster's, I expected something more than just the iPad game on a bigger touchscreen. That missed opportunity sparked this project, what if we translated touchscreen gestures into actual physical sword movements, creating a full-body, immersive experience that fundamentally reimagines how we interact with digital games?

Live motion capture demonstration showing real-time sword tracking in Unreal Engine 5

Platform

Motion Capture + Unreal Engine 5

Focus

Interface Design & Affordances

Input Method

Physical Sword Controller

Development Time

1 Week (Academic Project)

The Design Challenge

How do you translate instant, precise touchscreen swipes into physical sword movements? The core assignment was to take an existing digital interface and redesign its affordances through a different medium. Rather than making a 1:1 translation, this project explores what happens when you fundamentally change the interaction model.
